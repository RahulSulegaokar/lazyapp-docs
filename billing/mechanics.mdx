title: "How billing works"
description: "Starting a plan, upgrades, downgrades, cancelling, invoices, and how LazyApp records Meta's message charges."
---

Your plan is a Stripe subscription. Stripe holds your card, charges each renewal, retries failed payments and issues the invoices. LazyApp mirrors what Stripe reports, so **Settings → Billing** always shows the state Stripe has.

## Start a plan

1. Open **Settings → Billing** and choose Starter, Growth or Pro, monthly or yearly, with any add-ons.
2. Pay by card on Stripe Checkout. Plans are charged in INR.
3. The plan starts as soon as payment completes. Back in **Billing**, the page updates once Stripe confirms.

Your first plan payment is refundable in full if you ask within 7 days.

## Change a plan

**Billing** shows the exact amount for a change before you confirm it.

| Change | When it applies | What you pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upgrade: a bigger plan, more add-ons, or monthly to yearly | Now | The prorated difference, charged straight away |
| Downgrade: a smaller plan, fewer add-ons, or yearly to monthly | At the end of the current period | The new price from the next renewal |
| Cancel | At the end of the current period | Nothing more |

Upgrades apply now because you want the extra room now. If your card is declined, nothing changes: you stay on your current plan and are not charged.

Downgrades and cancellations wait for the period end because you have already paid for it. Until then you keep everything you paid for, and you can undo the downgrade or cancellation from **Billing**.

A downgrade has to fit what you use today. If you have more numbers connected or more members (counting pending invitations) than the smaller plan allows, LazyApp refuses the change and says what to remove first:

```text
You have 3 WhatsApp numbers connected, but Growth with these extras allows 1. Disconnect a number or add extra numbers first.
```

Features work differently. You can move to a plan without automations while you still have automations, for example. They stay in your workspace to open, pause, archive or delete, but you cannot create or change them in Live until you upgrade again. See [Feature gating](/billing/pricing-and-tiers#feature-gating).

## When a plan ends

After a cancellation takes effect, or when Stripe gives up on a failed payment, the workspace has no plan:

- Sending from your own number, automations and other plan features pause.
- Nothing is deleted. Your contacts, conversations, templates and settings stay.
- **Billing** shows **Choose a plan**. Pick one and everything works again.
- Test mode keeps working, as it does without a plan.

## Invoices

Stripe's invoice is the invoice of record. Each plan payment gets one, with a hosted page and a PDF. Stripe also emails a receipt for each successful payment.

- Open **Manage billing** in **Settings → Billing** to reach the Stripe Customer Portal, where you can download every invoice, change your card and cancel.
- Your billing details in **Settings → Billing** (legal name, billing email, address and optional GSTIN) are printed on your invoices.
- Billing emails go to the workspace owner, and to your billing email when it differs.

## Messages are not on your LazyApp invoice

Meta bills your messages directly, to the payment method on your WhatsApp Business Account, at Meta's rates. LazyApp adds 0%. Nothing about messages appears on your LazyApp invoice.

LazyApp still records every charged message on the usage ledger: its category, the contact's region, Meta's rate and whether Meta counted it as free. That record is for you to reconcile against Meta's invoice. **Usage** in the console shows it by number and category. See [Meta message billing](/billing/meta-message-billing).
