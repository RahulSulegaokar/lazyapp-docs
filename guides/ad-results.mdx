title: "Ad results"
description: "See the chats, leads and sales each Click-to-WhatsApp ad brought in, next to its name, spend and cost per result."
---

When someone taps a Click-to-WhatsApp ad on Facebook or Instagram and messages you, their first message tells LazyApp which ad it came from. When Meta later spots a lead or a purchase in that chat, it tells LazyApp too. **Analytics → Ad results** puts those together per ad, so you can see which ads bring customers and which only bring chats.

## What you see

| Column | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Chats | Messages that arrived from an ad |
| Leads | Chats Meta marked as a lead |
| Purchases and sales | Purchases Meta spotted in chats, with their value |
| Spend | What the ad cost, from your Meta ad account |
| Cost per result | Spend divided by purchases. With no purchases, by leads; with no leads, by chats |

Chats, leads and purchases need nothing extra. Leads and purchases need automatic events turned on when you connected WhatsApp (not available in the EU, UK or Japan).

Ad names, campaigns, spend and cost per result need your ad account connected.

## Connect your ad account

1. Open **Analytics → Ad results**.
2. Click **Connect ad account**.
3. Pick your ad account from the list, or paste its ID from the top left of Ads Manager.

LazyApp only reads ad names and spend. It never changes ads, budgets or audiences. Spend is refreshed once a day; **Refresh spend** fetches it now.

<Warning>
  Reading ad spend needs Meta's `ads_read` permission for LazyApp. Meta is still reviewing that request. Until it is approved, the page marks spend as **Waiting for Meta approval**, and chats, leads and purchases still count.
</Warning>

## Over the API

```bash
curl -G "https://lazyapp.in/v1/analytics/ads" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LAZYAPP_API_KEY" \
  --data-urlencode "days=30" \
  --data-urlencode "group_by=ad"
```

**Scope:** `messages:read`

| Parameter | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `days` | `7`, `30` (default) or `90` |
| `group_by` | `ad` (default) or `campaign` |

```json
{
  "data": [
    {
      "ad_id": "120211111111",
      "ad_name": "Diwali cake ad",
      "headline": "Order your Diwali cake",
      "campaign_id": "120200000000",
      "campaign_name": "Festive",
      "conversations": 42,
      "leads": 9,
      "purchases": 3,
      "revenue": 268500,
      "spend": 240000,
      "meta_conversations": 45,
      "result_kind": "purchase",
      "cost_per_result": 80000
    }
  ],
  "totals": { "conversations": 42, "leads": 9, "purchases": 3, "revenue": 268500, "meta_conversations": 45, "spend": 240000, "result_kind": "purchase", "cost_per_result": 80000 },
  "currency": "INR",
  "revenue_currency": "INR",
  "days": 30,
  "spend_available": true,
  "spend_unavailable_reason": null
}
```

Amounts are minor units: `240000` with `INR` is ₹2,400. `spend` and `cost_per_result` are `null` until an ad account is connected and readable; `spend_unavailable_reason` says why. `meta_conversations` is Meta's own count of conversations started, which uses a 7-day attribution window and can differ from `conversations`.

To act on a lead the moment it happens, subscribe to the [`conversion.detected`](/webhooks/events) webhook.
