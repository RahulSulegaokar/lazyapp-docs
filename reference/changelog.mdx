<Update label="2026-11-03" description="v1">

**In step with Meta's current WhatsApp docs (Graph API v26.0).** Sending, media, templates, Flows, numbers, webhooks, calling and groups were checked against Meta's live docs.

**Sending and media**

- `biz_opaque_callback_data` (up to 512 characters) on `POST /v1/messages`, `POST /v1/messages/direct-send` and the Meta-compatible send. It comes back on every message status webhook and on `GET /v1/messages/{uuid}`. See [Messages](/sending/messages#metas-tracking-string).
- Uploads follow Meta's limit for each kind: images 5 MB, video and audio 16 MB, documents 100 MB, stickers 100 KB (500 KB animated), with a plain-words refusal. See [Media](/sending/media#supported-formats-and-sizes).
- `GET /v1/media/{media}` adds `url`, Meta's short-lived download link.
- Every send is paced to Meta's per-number speed and per-person rate. Broadcasts wait out Meta's holds, pause when Meta pauses the template or reviews the account, and skip people at Meta's marketing limit; the campaign page says why. See [Rate limits](/reference/rate-limits#metas-per-number-speed) and [Broadcasts](/sending/broadcasts#pacing).
- More of Meta's error codes are explained in plain words in the console. See [Errors](/concepts/errors#metas-error-codes-in-plain-words).
- The Meta-compatible API returns Meta's real error body and HTTP status for a refused send, and `message_status` as Meta does. `GET …/{media}` returns Meta's download `url`. See [Migrating from Meta's Cloud API](/migrating/from-cloud-api#errors).
- `POST /v1/catalog/link-messages` sends your catalog as a link message, and `GET /v1/catalog/products` takes `search`. See [Catalog](/sending/catalog#catalog-links).

**Templates and Flows**

- `PUT /v1/templates/{template}` edits a template. `POST /v1/templates/archive`, `/unarchive` and `/bulk-delete` take up to 100 templates. `GET /v1/templates/{template}?refresh=true` reads one live from Meta. See [Managing templates](/sending/template-management#edit-a-template).
- Flow buttons name their flow by `flow_id`, `flow_name` or `flow_json`, with `flow_action` and `navigate_screen`; sends take `template.features.flow`. See [Templates](/sending/templates#flow-buttons).
- The console's visual Flow builder covers every Flow JSON 7.3 component. New flows use 7.3, and frozen versions are refused before upload. `POST /v1/flows/sync` and `GET /v1/flows/{flow}/assets` bring in flows made elsewhere. See [Flows](/guides/flows#build-a-flow-in-the-console).
- `metric=templates` reads Marketing Messages sends too, tagged with `product_type`. See [WhatsApp analytics](/guides/whatsapp-analytics#marketing-messages-results).

**Numbers and webhooks**

- Business usernames: `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /v1/connections/{uuid}/username`. See [Phone numbers](/guides/phone-numbers#business-username).
- `GET /v1/connections/{uuid}/account/owned-accounts`, and `service_pricing_policy` on accounts.
- `GET`, `POST` and `DELETE /v1/connections/{uuid}/webhook-subscription`. See [Receiving messages](/guides/phone-numbers#receiving-messages).
- A built-in welcome reply: `welcome_reply` on the conversation starters endpoints. See [Welcome reply](/guides/growth-tools#welcome-reply).
- New Meta webhook fields handled: `business_username_updates`, `standby`, `messaging_handovers` and `account_settings_update`. See [What Meta tells LazyApp](/webhooks/whatsapp-fields).

**Calling and groups**

- Calling (coming soon): SIP setup with `POST /v1/connections/{uuid}/calling-settings/sip-password`, voicemail `timeout_seconds` of 0 to 30, permission requests by template outside the 24-hour window, and TURN for the inbox softphone. See [Calling](/guides/calling).
- `metric=groups` takes one group, by `group` uuid or Meta id, for up to 90 days. See [Group analytics](/guides/whatsapp-analytics#group-analytics).

**Removed**

- `metric=conversation` on `GET /v1/analytics/whatsapp`. Meta stopped serving conversation analytics on 1 November 2026; it now answers `422`. Use `metric=pricing`.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-30" description="v1">

**Claim your customers' replies.** Platform customers take `manages_customer_replies` on create and `PATCH`, and every customer object returns it. When it is `true`, LazyApp's AI reply assistant never replies on that customer's numbers, an assistant they had switched on is switched off, and the console locks the setting. Use it when your platform answers contacts with its own bot, so nobody gets two replies. See [Answer your customers' contacts yourself](/platform/overview#answer-your-customers-contacts-yourself).

**Every platform event names the customer.** Template, contact consent, `customer.connected`, `setup_link.failed`, `customer.usage_alert` and customer lifecycle events now carry the `customer` envelope, so you can route them by `customer.external_id` like message events.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-23" description="v1">

**Flat plans.** Flat monthly plans (Starter, Growth and Pro) replace the per-message charge. LazyApp adds 0% to Meta's rates: Meta bills each message to the business's own payment method, and the plan is what pays LazyApp. Test mode stays free. See [Plans and limits](/billing/pricing-and-tiers).

**Live keys need Pro.** A `sk_live_` key on a workspace below Pro gets `402` with `code: plan_limit_reached`. A broadcast past the month's live broadcast sends, or a number past the plan's numbers, returns `422` with a plain message. Test keys are never plan-limited. See [Errors](/concepts/errors#402-plan-limit-reached).

**Platform customers.** `/v1/platform/customers` no longer stores or returns `markup_bps`, `pricing_mode`, `category_markups` or `platform_fee`. Values you still send are ignored, and the request succeeds. Customer usage no longer includes `wholesale_total` or `marked_up_total`.

**Fixes from building real apps on the API.**

- Free-form replies in an open conversation count against the contact's last message (10 each), not the hourly per-recipient cap, so a bot no longer stops answering mid-conversation. See [Rate limits](/reference/rate-limits#send-velocity-abuse-guards).
- `POST /v1/flows` returns the data-endpoint `endpoint_secret` once, and `POST /v1/flows/{flow}/rotate-endpoint-secret` replaces it. See [Flows](/guides/flows#verify-the-signature).
- `flow.response_received` reaches endpoints restricted to the number the flow was completed on.
- `PATCH /v1/webhooks/{webhook}` updates an endpoint in place. The OpenAPI `subscribed_events` enum now lists every event.
- Message webhooks carry `contact.profile_name`. `commerce.order` is in the [event reference](/webhooks/events).
- A plain "yes" (in any language) no longer restores marketing consent after STOP. START and UNSTOP still do.
- OTP: refusals carry a `code`, waiting is a `429` with `Retry-After`, the per-IP rule uses the optional `client_ip`, send and verify have separate limits, `language` is accepted, a code needs an authentication template outside the 24-hour window, and a locked number stays locked for the right code too. See [Verify](/sending/verify#limits).
- Contact `attributes` are merged instead of replaced.
- Segments filter tags by `names` or `uuids`. Broadcasts validate `variable_map` and document `header_media`.
- `POST /v1/events` takes `"deliver": false` to record an event without sending, and `/v1/event-definitions` defines your own event types.
- `PATCH /v1/conversations/{id}` assigns, sets status and tags; `/v1/conversations/{id}/notes` adds internal notes.
- `POST /v1/templates` accepts `header.example_url` and `carousel_cards[].example_url` instead of a separate sample upload.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-09-01" description="v1">

**Retrieve a message.** `GET /v1/messages/{uuid}` returns LazyApp's local message record (status, timestamps, body, metadata). Scope `messages:read`. Prefer webhooks in production; this is for confirmation and debugging.

**Customer API key scopes.** `POST /v1/platform/customers/{customer}/api-keys` accepts an optional `scopes` array and returns the granted scopes with the plaintext key.

**Platform guides.** Docs now cover the multi-tenant model plus tenant messaging, usage dashboards, and inbox recipes under Platforms.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-25" description="v1">

**Call history API.** `GET /v1/calls` and `GET /v1/calls/{call}` return WhatsApp call records (direction, status, duration, related conversation). Scope `messages:read`. Live WebRTC answer is not included.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-25" description="v1">

**Automation packs.** `PUT /v1/automation-packs/provision` seeds event-to-template mappings, starter workflows and Flows for a vertical pack (starting with `real_estate`). See [automation packs](/sending/automation-packs).

**Event mapping API.** `GET /v1/event-mappings` lists workspace mappings. `PUT /v1/event-mappings/provision` creates or updates them from a pack or an explicit list.

**Capabilities fields.** `GET /v1/capabilities` returns `connection_uuid`, `quality_rating` and `messaging_limit_tier` per connection.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-22" description="v1">

**Webhook resume and secret rotation.** Paused endpoints can be turned back on with `POST /v1/webhooks/{webhook}/resume`. Lost signing secrets can be rotated with `POST /v1/webhooks/{webhook}/rotate-secret` without deleting the endpoint. Listing now includes `pause_reason`.

**Inbound media download.** `GET /v1/messages/{message}/media` returns the bytes of media a contact sent in. Returns `409` while the download is still queued.

**Contact consent API.** `POST /v1/contacts/{contact}/consent` records consent collected outside LazyApp — for example from a website opt-in box. Granting marketing consent lifts a matching suppression.

**Starter template sets.** `GET /v1/templates/sets` lists the template sets this installation supports. Pass `template_set` when creating a setup link.

**`customers:act` scope.** Platforms can act on a customer's behalf with `X-LazyApp-Customer` without holding `customers:write`.

**Stable send refusal codes.** Refused sends now include a `code` — `customer_service_window_closed`, `contact_suppressed`, `marketing_opt_out`, or `no_connection`. Contacts report `service_window` on read.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-19" description="v1 · breaking, pre-launch">

**One shape for one object.** The Platform API wrapped single objects in a `data`
envelope while every other single-object response was bare. All of them are bare now;
`data` wraps lists only. See [conventions](/concepts/conventions#response-shape).

**Every `DELETE` answers `204`.** Four endpoints previously returned `200` with
`{"deleted": true}` or `{"cancelled": true}`. A successful delete has no body.

**`GET /v1/workflows` cursor-paginates** like every other list, so a workspace with
more than 100 workflows can read all of them.

<Note>
  These three are breaking, and they are shipping before the API has integrators.
  Nothing after this point changes an existing response shape inside `v1` — see
  [versioning](/reference/versioning).
</Note>

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-19" description="v1">

**Idempotency on every write.** `Idempotency-Key` is now honoured on all `/v1` write endpoints, not just messages and events. A retry replays the recorded response; reusing a key with a different body is refused. See [idempotency](/concepts/idempotency).

**Cursor pagination everywhere.** Broadcasts, events, media, templates, flows, scheduled messages and webhook endpoints now paginate like contacts and conversations, so a caller with more than one page can reach all of it.

**Media access history.** `GET /v1/media/{media}` returns `access_history` — who read, uploaded or deleted a file, when, and from where. The history outlives the asset.

**Events accept workspace-defined types and contact recipients.** `type` now validates against the event definitions in your workspace, and `recipient.type: contact_id` addresses a contact directly.

**`contact_information_request`.** Ask a contact for a phone number inside a conversation and have the reply attach to their record.

**Added `GET /v1/capabilities`** so clients can check what a connection supports instead of hard-coding assumptions.

**Scheduled sending.** `send_at` and `ttl_seconds` on `POST /v1/messages`, plus `GET` and `DELETE /v1/messages/scheduled`.

**Added** `/v1/templates`, `/v1/contacts`, `/v1/conversations`, `/v1/workflows`, `/v1/broadcasts`, `/v1/webhooks` and `/v1/flows`.

**Added the Meta-compatible proxy** at `/meta/whatsapp/{version}/...`. See [migrating](/migrating/from-cloud-api).

**Added `POST /v1/integrations/{source}/events`** for external systems.

**Every response now carries** `X-RateLimit-*` and `X-LazyApp-Api-Version`.

</Update>
